STAGE 1

ARCHITECTURAL AND PLANNING CONCEPT
(DRAFT)

The main objective of this stage is to combine all your requirements identified through the brief with the initial site information: dimensions, area, terrain, orientation, sunlight exposure, and other key factors. As a result, we create a three-dimensional model of the future home, including its layout, technical solutions, and overall visual concept.

OPERATING ALGORITHM:

Implementation period3 MONTHS
1

Idea

Based on the brief, we create the first concept sketch of the house, which is then refined and developed through multiple iterations. We organize weekly meetings or calls to review the current design version with you, discuss adjustments, and gradually bring the project closer to its final vision.

2

Functional zoning

Once we have the first approved concept for the house, we begin developing the functional layout and positioning the building on the site. At this stage, we carefully analyze the relationship between all spaces and organize circulation so the house works practically and comfortably.

3

Interactive 3D model

The next step is the creation of a three-dimensional model of the house. We develop a detailed 3D model that allows us to resolve mass, volumes, façade composition, and overall architectural appearance.

4

Planning solution

Next, based on the previous stages, we develop a refined floor plan with furniture placement, ensuring that all dimensions, clearances, and proportions are accurate. We also integrate the layout into a three-dimensional 3D model, allowing you to visually explore the interior.

5

Visualizations

We provide a detailed 3D model or visualization team, who use it to create photorealistic images of the house with its real surroundings, façade materials and technical solutions.

6

Detail and packaging of the project into an album

As a final result, we compile all the developed materials into a comprehensive project album that provides a complete understanding of the house's design, functionality, and final appearance.

RESULTS OF THE 1ST STAGE

Thus, you receive not just a well-developed architectural concept—a sketch project that can be further developed into detailed construction documentation—but also an estimated budget for the realization of the proposed design.

STAGE 2

ARCHITECTURAL AND PLANNING CONCEPT
(CONSTRUCTION PROJECT)

The main goal of this stage is to provide clear instructions and explanations on how to transform our concept into reality. The construction project is divided into two main sections, completed by two different specialists: architectural solutions and design solutions.

MAIN SECTIONS:

Implementation period3 MONTHS
1

Constructive solutions

This section is handled by the structural engineer, who calculates the loads on the building and develops all structural elements. The main task is to ensure the safety, durability, and reliability of the house for many years while keeping the chosen solutions within the established budget.

To begin the structural design, we need geological data from your site. You can provide this information yourself through specialized companies or request this service from us for an additional fee.

2

Architectural solutions

An architect works alongside the structural engineer, developing our preliminary design into a complete set of technical drawings, explanations, and documentation. Their job is to define how the house will be built according to the original concept.

After the architect completes the documentation, you receive a detailed schedule of the main materials required for construction, allowing you to understand the quantities needed for the project.

RESULTS OF THE 2ND STAGE

The result of this stage is a complete construction project—a detailed guide explaining how to build the house, what materials to use, and how to execute complex technical solutions. Such documentation helps optimize construction costs and provides precise material quantities.

STAGE 3

ENGINEERING NETWORKS

The goal of this stage is to provide a clear understanding of the engineering systems and technologies that will be implemented in the home. We recommend developing the utility networks section together with us, as part of a turnkey construction project.

  • heating
  • ventilation
  • water supply
  • sewerage
  • electrical systems

COMPOSITION OF THE ENGINEERING PROJECT:

Implementation period3 MONTHS
1

Study and analysis of the conditions of implementation

The development of the utility networks section begins with a detailed analysis of the conditions required for installation of the future technical systems. We coordinate the work of specialist engineers who study the initial data, evaluate possible solutions, and develop the most efficient engineering systems tailored to your project.

2

Detailed BIM model

All selected engineering concepts are incorporated into our digital 3D model and carefully reviewed by the architect to ensure proper coordination between all involved specialists. This verification process helps identify and eliminate possible conflicts, overlaps, or technical inconsistencies between the different systems.

RESULTS OF THE 3RD STAGE

As a result, you receive a complete and integrated design package for all engineering systems of your home. An engineering project makes construction predictable and coordinated while helping optimize the overall cost of realization.

STAGE 4

CONSTRUCTION OF THE FACILITY

This stage involves monitoring construction processes and deadlines on our part, relieving you of the need to do it yourself and “live” at the construction site. There are 2 options for our participation: customer service and copyright supervision.

SECTION COMPOSITION:

Implementation period6 MONTHS
1

Customer service

At this stage, we take full responsibility for the coordination and management of the construction process. We prepare a detailed estimate and a complete specification of the works planned on site for your approval. Once approved, we organize the project into clear phases with defined deadlines and coordinate the work of all contractors involved.

  • architect-designer who ensures the construction remains faithful to the approved design;
  • construction manager who oversees daily operations and supervises the work on site;
  • independent technical supervision specialists who provide an objective assessment of construction quality.
2

Author's supervision

At this stage, our involvement and supervision of the construction process are more limited, as the majority of coordination and communication with contractors are handled directly by you. Our role is to verify that construction follows the architectural solutions developed in the project.

STAGE 5

INTERIOR DESIGN

The main goal of interior design is to create a distinctive and comfortable environment that responds to your functional needs, aesthetic preferences, and lifestyle. As a result, you receive a carefully planned and ergonomic interior where every element is designed for your comfort, practicality, and daily enjoyment.

WORK ON INTERIOR DESIGN IS BUILT IN SEVERAL STAGES:

Implementation period3 MONTHS
1

Meeting-acquaintance

Together with our designer, we hold an initial discussion to understand your preferences, expectations, and vision for the future interior. At this stage, we also gather the necessary project information.

2

Conclusion of a contract

Next, based on your requirements, we prepare a personalized proposal and finalize the agreement. Once the contract is approved and signed, we proceed with the interior design development.

3

Concept

At this stage, we bring together your preferences and concepts with the existing project conditions. We explore different layout solutions, define the architectural design and functional logic, and create a visual direction for every room.

4

Photorealistic visualizations.

We provide the developed interior concept to our visualization specialists, who transform it into photorealistic images of your future space. At this stage, we also finalize the selection of finishing materials and technical solutions.

5

Detail and packaging of the project into an album

As a final step, we compile all the developed materials into a complete design album that provides you with a clear understanding of the interior concept, its details, and final appearance.
