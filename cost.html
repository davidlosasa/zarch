Architectural design and interior space design

We prepared a transparent online calculator so you can estimate the cost of architectural design and interior space design before the first meeting. The preliminary figure is based on typology, area and the depth of documentation you need.

Use it as a working budget for a house, an apartment, or a mixed-use interior. The result is not a contract — it is a clear starting point for a conversation with the studio.

CALCULATION COST

PRELIMINARY ASSESSMENT OF THE COST OF DESIGNING A HOUSE AND DEVELOPING A DESIGN PROJECT FOR THE INTERIOR OF AN APARTMENT

The calculator considers the type of object, the area, the composition of the team and the set of drawings required for construction. Interior design can be estimated together with architecture or as a separate package.

A preliminary figure helps you plan financing early. It is updated after the brief, when the real constraints of the site and the programme are known.

If you already have a plot or an apartment, send the area and a short description — we will return a first range and propose a meeting to start the individual project.
