ABOUT ZARCH

01 ARCHITECTS

01Architects is a collaborative architectural practice founded on July 8, 2021, born from the belief that great ideas emerge when creative minds come together. What began as a simple initiative to develop skills in architectural software quickly evolved into a collective dedicated to design exploration and innovation.

The name 01Architects symbolizes unity — many individuals forming one vision. The practice encompasses collaboration, experimentation, and continuous learning within architecture and digital design.

Driven by creativity and technical mastery, 01Architects aims to cultivate new talents, explore contemporary architectural ideas, and contribute meaningful design solutions to the built environment.
