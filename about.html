ABOUT ZARCH

01 ARCHITECTS

ZARCH 01 Architects is an independent studio working across architecture, interiors, landscape and visualization. We treat every commission as a long conversation between place, programme and the people who will inhabit the work.

From civic buildings to private houses, the office looks for a clear idea that can survive construction, climate and time. Drawings, models and images are tools — the project itself is the beginning of a building’s life.

Architecture with an idea

We start from a single, readable concept and test it against structure, light and use. The result should be specific to its site, not a style applied from elsewhere.

Project — is just the beginning

Delivery does not end at the last drawing. We stay with the client through construction and the first years of occupation, so the building can keep the idea that made it.

BUILT TO LAST.
DESIGNED TO MATTER.
